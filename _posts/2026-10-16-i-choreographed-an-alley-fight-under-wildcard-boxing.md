**May 25, 2018.** Hollywood.

I posted my **first headshot**.

Not of me.

Of **David Wachs**.

I shot it at **Wildcard Boxing Club** right after a fight I had choreographed and filmed with **Jasper Jeon**.

![Dramatic night portrait of actor David Wachs in a leather jacket, looking over his shoulder](/assets/images/2026-10-16/david-wachs-headshot.jpg)
*My first headshot: David Wachs, shot at Wildcard Boxing Club after the fight. Photo: McKay Stewart, [shared on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1000078753685389312).*

## Wildcard Gym

Wildcard is Freddie Roach's gym on Vine Street in Hollywood.

I train Western boxing there. That detail sits on my [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1229683/bio/) bio with my other martial arts.

On **June 1, 2018** I posted a simple line:

> "Nothing like some boxing at Wildcard in Hollywood"

Hashtags: **#funburn**, **#wildcardselfie**, **#freddyroachstyle**.

No photo that day. Just the burn.

A few weeks later I was still playing with a new Nikon at home. I posted a bright "smelfie" and asked what people thought.

![McKay Stewart bright high-key selfie experimenting with a new Nikon](/assets/images/2026-10-16/nikon-smelfie.jpg)
*Experimenting with the new Nikon at home. Photo: McKay Stewart, [June 29, 2018 on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1012782021473550336).*

That same camera habit showed up under the gym.

## The alley under the gym

We shot the fight **beneath Wildcard Boxing Gym**.

Alley. Night. Concrete. Overhead lights.

**David Wachs** and **Jasper Jeon** (also tagged as Jasper Howard Jeon / @jasperheon) went head to head.

I did four jobs on it:

- Fight choreography
- Camera
- Edit
- Direction

They learned the choreography and we shot it in about **one hour**.

**Marc Mellens** did sound design and score. He also helped with color.

**Christy-Anne Lopez** appears in the piece too.

I shot it myself on a **Nikon**.

![David Wachs in a leather jacket and white tank in a night alley, from the Alley Brawl short](/assets/images/2026-10-16/alley-brawl-cover.jpg)
*Night alley look from the finished short. Frame from [David Wachs vs. Jasper Jeon — Alley Brawl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MvandcWglX0).*

## Same crew, later fight: Down Tempo

**December 2020.** I came back with the same two actors.

Piece title: **"Down Tempo."**

Again starring **Jasper Jeon** and **David Wachs**.

This time I wrote it too.

Credits I listed:

- Written by me
- Directed by me
- Fight choreographed by me
- Shot by me
- Edited by me
- Sound design and scoring by **Marc Mellens** again

I posted the clip on X on **December 16, 2020** and said we crushed it. They were easy to work with. The fight looked hard. The set felt simple and street.

## Why this stuck with me

I like fights that feel real.

Not big CG. Not a giant stage.

Just two actors who learn the beat, a Nikon, an alley under a boxing gym, and a clock that says **one hour**.

Wildcard taught me the boxing. The alley let me put that training into a camera.

David and Jasper made it look easy. Marc made it sound mean.

If you only watch one, start with the [Alley Brawl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MvandcWglX0). Then hit [Down Tempo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpz1VZCGXZE).
