## Mueller after dark

**October 16, 2023.** Night session at **Mueller Skate Park** in Austin.

My caption: "Weeeeee best calorie burn ever haha." Hashtags: **#skateordietrying** and **#odetojoy**.

Mueller is special. It's home to the **first all-concrete pump track in Texas**, per [KXAN](https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/skate-park-and-pump-track-rolls-open-in-mueller/).

What's a pump track? It's a loop of rollers and banked turns. You don't push with your foot. You "pump" with your legs, up and down, to keep your speed. It's harder than it looks. And it's a serious leg workout.

The park opened to the public on **August 11, 2022**. Its grand opening was **October 21, 2022**, per [SPA Skateparks](https://www.spaskateparks.com/projects/city-of-austin-mueller-skatepark/), the company that built it. The skate park and its **two** pump tracks make up about **15,000** square feet of terrain.

Best part? The lights stay on until **10 p.m.** That's why so many of my Mueller clips are at night.

## 2026: House Park in slow motion

**April 29, 2026.** This time I was behind the camera too.

I took my **Nikon Z6III** to **House Park** in downtown Austin. That's the same park where I caught my Christmas air in 2022. Its official name is the **Heath Eiland and Morgan Moss BMX Skate Park**, per the [City of Austin](https://services.austintexas.gov/department/skate-parks).

The test: **120 frames per second**. At night. Under stadium lights.

My caption: "No optical flow, no AI. Just raw frames, stadium lights, and a SmallRig 60."

Why does that matter? A lot of slow motion today is faked. Software or AI guesses the "in-between" frames. That's called optical flow. Mine is all real frames.

Here's the quick math. Normal movie speed is **24** frames a second. Shoot **120**, play it back at **24**, and every second of action stretches to **5** seconds. Nikon lists the Z6III at up to **120p** in 4K and up to **240p** in Full HD, per [Nikon's spec sheet](https://imaging.nikon.com/imaging/lineup/mirrorless/z6_3/).
