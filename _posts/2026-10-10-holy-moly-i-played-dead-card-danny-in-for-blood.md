Holy moly. **August 2014.** I posted a set photo and six words that still make me grin:

> "Just wrapped #forbloodmovie … good times!"

I tagged co-stars **April Scott** and **Rick Mora**. A few months later I dropped four character posters and a teaser. Then the short hit festivals.

Twelve years later, here's the full story. Who I played. Who else was at that poker table. And the award the film actually won.

## What is For Blood?

*For Blood* is an **11-minute** short. Genres: **Western** and **Fantasy**. It came out in **2015**.

The setup is simple and wild.

A group of friends sit down for a poker game. Someone says a Wild West card game would have higher stakes. They splash the pot. A new world shows up. Suddenly they're somewhere else, some*when* else — playing **for blood**.

That's the whole pitch. Friends. Cards. A splash. Another century.

You can look it up on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3917844/) (tt3917844). Rotten Tomatoes lists it as an **11-minute** Western/Fantasy short. Streaming date there: **May 1, 2020**.

## Who made it

**Chadwick Pelletier** wrote it and directed it. Some listings also credit **John T. Connor** as co-director and as director of photography.

If that DP name rings a bell: John T. Connor also shot my short [*West Side Swordy*](/2026/10/08/holy-moly-west-side-swordy/). Small world. Good world.

Production company: **CoffeeRing Entertainment**. Also connected in credits: Veritas Film & Television and The Hollywood Index.

Producers included **Julie Helton** and **April Scott** (associate producer). **Jeremy Scott Glenn** is listed as co-producer. **Mark Hulsey** as co-executive producer.

## My role: Dead Card Danny

I played **Dead Card Danny**.

He's the gambler at the table. Suit. Bowler hat. Cards in hand. The guy who looks like he knows which way the deck is stacked — and doesn't care.

CoffeeRing cut character posters for the main players. Mine said it plain across the frame:

**MCKAY STEWART — DEAD CARD DANNY — ANTE UP 2015**

![Dead Card Danny character poster](/assets/images/2026-10-10/forblood-1.jpg)
*My Dead Card Danny character poster. Bowler, suit, one card ready. Shared [on X, Nov 4, 2014](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/529663056234950656).*

Playing a cardsharp in a fantasy Western is a blast. You get period wardrobe, hard lighting, and a table full of people who all look like they could end you. Then the magic kicks in and the stakes stop being chips.

## Festival win: San Diego, 2015

This part is on the public record.

At the **2015 San Diego International Film Festival** (Sept 30–Oct 4, 2015), *For Blood* won the **Kumeyaay Award**.

That award is the festival's prize for best Native American film. Directors listed with the win: **Chadwick Pelletier** and **J.T. Connor**.

You can check the festival's award winners list yourself. It's the verified one. (Some bios float other festival claims. I'm sticking to what the San Diego winners page shows.)

**Runtime:** 11 minutes.
**Year:** 2015.
**Award:** Kumeyaay Award, San Diego International Film Festival.

That's a short film doing real work.

## Why this one still matters to me

**2014** was a busy year.

In **April** I was stunt doubling **Scott Wolf** on TNT's *Perception*. (I wrote about that [here](/2026/10/09/holy-moly-i-doubled-scott-wolf-on-perception/).)

In **August** I wrapped *For Blood*.

In **November** I booked a role on *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* (that story is coming).

Same stretch of months: stunt work, a fantasy Western short, and Marvel. That's the hustle. You take the fight day. You take the poker-table day. You take the next call.

*For Blood* is small on paper. **Eleven minutes.** One room that turns into another world. But the craft was real. The wardrobe was real. The posters were real. The festival plaque was real.

And Dead Card Danny? Still one of my favorite character names I've ever gotten to wear on a poster.
