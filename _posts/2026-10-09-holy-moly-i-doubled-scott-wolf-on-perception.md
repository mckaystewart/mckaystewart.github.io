## What was Perception?

*Perception* was a crime drama on **TNT**. It ran **3 seasons**, from **July 9, 2012** to **March 17, 2015**. ABC Studios made it.

**Eric McCormack** starred. Yes, Will from *Will & Grace*.

He played **Dr. Daniel Pierce**. Pierce is a brilliant neuroscience professor in Chicago. He also has **paranoid schizophrenia**. The FBI calls him in on its weirdest cases.

The twist? Pierce sees people who aren't there. Sometimes those visions help him spot the clue everyone else missed.

**Rachael Leigh Cook** played FBI agent **Kate Moretti**, his former student. **LeVar Burton**, **Arjay Smith** and **Kelly Rowan** were in the cast too.

TNT canceled it in **November 2014**. The last episodes aired in early 2015.

## Who is Scott Wolf?

If you watched TV in the '90s, you know him. Scott Wolf was **Bailey Salinger** on *Party of Five*. That show ran **1994 to 2000**. He was in **142 episodes**.

After that he kept working. A lot.

- ***Everwood*** (2004–2006): Dr. Jake Hartman.
- ***The Nine*** (2006–2007): Jeremy Kates.
- ***V*** (2009–2011): Chad Decker, a TV journalist.
- ***The Night Shift*** (2014–2017): Dr. Scott Clemmens.
- ***Nancy Drew*** (2019–2023): Carson Drew, Nancy's dad. He directed **2** episodes too.
- ***Doc*** (2025–2026): Dr. Richard Miller.

On *Perception* he played **Donnie Ryan**. Donnie is Kate's ex. He's a sharp, fast-rising Assistant U.S. Attorney. *The Hollywood Reporter* called it a major recurring role when he joined in **2013**. He was in **25 episodes** through 2015.

My job: make sure Donnie could take a hit without Scott taking one.

## What a stunt double actually does

Here's the deal. A stunt double steps in when a scene gets risky.

A fight. A fall. A car chase. Anything where the actor could get hurt.

- **You match the actor.** Same height range. Same build. Same hair. Same wardrobe. In our case: orange jumpsuits.
- **You learn the fight.** The stunt coordinator plans every move. You rehearse until it's clean and safe.
- **You sell it.** The camera stays wide or catches you from behind. Then the actor steps back in for the close-up.

A **stunt driver** handles the car stuff. Fast turns. Near misses. Hard stops. The goal is to make it look wild while keeping it totally under control.

When it works, nobody watching should notice you. That's the whole point.

## My credits on Perception

You can look these up yourself:

- **Metacritic** lists me on *Perception* as **"stunt double: Scott Wolf / stunt driver"** for **2 episodes** in **2014**.
- **IMDb** credits me as a **stunt driver** on the season 3 episode **"Dirty"** ([full credits](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3765302/fullcredits/)).

"Dirty" aired **August 19, 2014**. **LeVar Burton** directed it. Short version: Donnie lands in a compromising spot and calls Pierce for help. Then the evidence starts piling up against him.

So the timeline goes:

1. **April 2014.** I'm on set. I post the photo.
2. **August 2014.** The episode airs with my name in the credits.

You sweat in spring. It airs in summer. That's TV.

## How I got ready for this

Stunt doubling doesn't start on set. It starts years before.

- **7 martial arts.** Wushu Kung Fu, Jeet Kune Do, Muay Thai, Tae Kwon Do, Kali, Savate and Jiu-Jitsu.
- **Boxing.** Trained at Wildcard Boxing.
- **Weapons.** Broadsword, Wushu staff, Chinese broadsword and fencing.
- **Real fights.** I've fought Muay Thai in the ring.
- **Gymnastics and dance.** Years of musical theater, including *West Side Story*.
- **Precision driving.** Trained by **Rick Seaman**, one of the best-known stunt driving teachers around.

Scott and I kept in touch on X. In **2021** I told him to do a fight or fall off a cliff so I could double him again. Offer still stands, Scott.
