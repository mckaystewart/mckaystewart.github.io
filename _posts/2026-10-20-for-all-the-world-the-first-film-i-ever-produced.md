**January 12, 2021.** I posted a short video with two hashtags up front.

**#firstprojectIproduced. #ForAlltheWorld.**

I wrote that it was "so much fun." I thanked the people who made it with me: **Yulia Popova**, **Ravi Vora** and **Pat Fogarty**.

That video was **46 seconds** long. But the story behind it goes back more than a decade.

![McKay Stewart in a dark suit and Yulia Popova in a black dress stand close on a bridge rail above a downtown Los Angeles freeway at dusk, light trails streaking below and lit towers behind them](/assets/images/2026-10-20/fatw-freeway-bridge-still.jpg)
*Yulia Popova and me above the freeway. Still by Ravi Vora from For All the World, [posted on X January 18, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1351301274256384002).*

## The pitch

Here's how Ravi described it on his YouTube channel:

> "What would you do if you knew love meant you could never be safe?"

The setup: the daughter of a crime family and an FBI agent fall for each other. Then they run from a powerful crime lord.

Ravi's own words for it: "A James Bond-esque action movie."

Suits. Gowns. City lights. A chase. That's my kind of project.

![A man in a suit and a woman in a black dress run hand in hand past a white van in a parking garage](/assets/images/2026-10-20/fatw-running-past-van.jpg)
*On the run. Frame from the ['For All The World' trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoW-v659C7k) by Ravi Vora.*

## Who made it

The trailer credits are short and clear:

- **Directed by** Ravi Vora
- **Starring** me and Yulia Popova
- **Produced by** me and Ravi Vora

Ravi also did the editing, the score, the ADR (that's re-recorded dialogue) and the special effects.

My own **46-second** cut, the one I posted in 2021, lists a few more names on screen:

- **Edited by** Patrick Fogarty
- **Make up by** Katelin Gan
- **Created by** me

So this was a small team. Really small.

![The words FOR ALL THE WORLD in glowing silver letters over a blue blur of light](/assets/images/2026-10-20/fatw-title-card.jpg)
*The title card. Frame from the ['For All The World' trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoW-v659C7k).*

## A first for both of us

This was the **first project I ever produced**. I've said so in my posts more than once.

It was a first for Ravi too. In his trailer notes, he called it "my first directing project."

He also said he did the editing, effects and audio himself, all at once. His advice on that:

> "Learning to edit, handle special effects, audio, and all other aspects of filmmaking in one go, is not recommended. :)"

Fair. But look at what came out of it.

![McKay Stewart, smiling in an open white shirt, in a crowded outdoor market with Yulia Popova beside him](/assets/images/2026-10-20/fatw-market-smile.jpg)
*In the crowd. Frame from the ['For All The World' trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoW-v659C7k).*

## Who is Ravi Vora?

Today Ravi is a **commercial director and photographer** in Los Angeles.

His website lists film and commercial work for **Nike**, **Acura**, **Qantas**, **Mastercard**, **Fitbit** and **Microsoft**.

Back in **2016**, the design site *Fubiz* featured his travel and people photos. It named brands he'd shot for, like **Ford**, **Beats by Dre**, **HP**, **Nike** and **Alfa Romeo**.

So when I call him "uber talented" in my post, I mean it.

## The camera that made it possible

The trailer notes say it was **shot on a Canon 5D Mark II**. Audio came from a **Zoom H4n** recorder. It was edited in **Final Cut Pro** and **After Effects**.

Why does the camera matter?

Canon announced the 5D Mark II on **September 17, 2008**. It was a still camera. But it was the **first full-frame DSLR to shoot 1080p video**.

That changed indie film. People call it the "DSLR revolution."

Big shows used it too:

- **2009:** *Saturday Night Live* shot its season **35** opening titles on it, along with a Canon 7D.
- **May 17, 2010:** Fox aired the *House* episode "Help Me," shot entirely on the 5D Mark II.

Ravi's trailer went up on YouTube on **August 6, 2010**. Same era. Same camera.

A camera you could carry gave a two-person producing team a big-movie look.

## Still in my reel

**February 10, 2025.** I posted my updated demo reel.

The last scenes before my name card? *For All the World.* The tux. The dress. The city at night.

Fifteen years on, it still earns its spot.

## The short version

- **Sept 17, 2008:** Canon announces the 5D Mark II.
- **Aug 6, 2010:** Ravi posts the *For All the World* trailer. His first directing project. My first as a producer.
- **Nov 6, 2011:** My **46-second** cut goes up on YouTube.
- **Jan 12, 2021:** I post it on X. #firstprojectIproduced.
- **Jan, Aug and Oct 2021:** I share Ravi's stills.
- **Feb 10, 2025:** It closes out my demo reel.

Every producer starts somewhere. I started here, with good people and one great camera.

Thanks, Ravi. Thanks, Yulia, Pat and Katelin.
