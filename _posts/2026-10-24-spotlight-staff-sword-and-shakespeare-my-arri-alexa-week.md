## First, the camera

The **Arri Alexa** is a digital movie camera made by the German company Arri.

The first Alexa came out in **April 2010**. A smaller version, the **Alexa Mini**, was announced on **February 24, 2015**.

It's a big deal in Hollywood. One study found the Alexa was the main camera on **more than 70%** of the top 100 grossing films since 2016. Cinematographer Roger Deakins used it on *Skyfall* and *1917*.

So yes, I was thrilled to shoot on one. I tagged it in almost every post: **#arrialexa**.

## Who was Hotspur?

He was a real man.

His name was **Sir Henry Percy**. He was born on **May 20, 1364**, in Northumberland, in the north of England.

The Scots gave him the nickname **"Hotspur."** It was a nod to how fast he moved and how ready he was to attack.

He became a **Knight of the Garter** at **24**. He fought the Scots on the border and the French in the Hundred Years' War.

Then he turned against King Henry IV. He was killed at the **Battle of Shrewsbury** on **July 21, 1403**. He was **39**.

In Shakespeare's ***Henry IV, Part 1***, Hotspur dies in a one-on-one fight with **Prince Hal**, the future King Henry V. Shakespeare made them the same age. In real life, Hotspur was **22 years older**.

Big actors have played him. **Sean Connery** did it for the BBC in **1960**. **Tom Glynn-Carney** played him in ***The King*** (2019).

And yes, the soccer club **Tottenham Hotspur** is named after him.
