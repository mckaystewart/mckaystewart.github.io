**January 1, 2021.** New Year's Day.

I posted a teaser for a project I was developing with director **Garrett Warren**.

It's called ***GLITCH***.

One line from that post: "Me in the hoodie."

![McKay Stewart in a dark hoodie stands in the open back doors of a white van inside a parking garage, in a frame from the GLITCH teaser](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-hoodie-van.jpg)
*Me in the hoodie, in the back of the van. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI), written and directed by Garrett Warren.*

Here's the story of a **2-and-a-half-minute** teaser, the director who made it, and the jobs that came after.

## Who is Garrett Warren?

He's the writer and director of *GLITCH*. Before that, he worked on some of the biggest action movies around.

Garrett was the **stunt coordinator** on James Cameron's *Avatar* (**2009**). He ran the stunts on *Alita: Battle Angel* (**2019**). He was **second unit director** on *Logan* (**2017**). That's the team that shoots a lot of the action.

His [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0912836/) lists **127** stunt credits. His bio there calls him an ex-world champion martial artist. It says he's taught fight work to Hugh Jackman, Zoe Saldaña, Chris Evans and Idris Elba.

He also told [Kung-fu Kingdom](https://kungfukingdom.com/interview-with-garrett-warren/) this about making *Avatar*:

> "'Avatar' changed me not just as a filmmaker, but as a human being."

So yes. When Garrett writes a fight, you show up.

## The goal

**December 24, 2019.** I posted my goals for 2020.

Number one: get *GLITCH*, with Garrett, picked up by **Lightstorm Entertainment**.

That's the production company [James Cameron founded in **1990**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightstorm_Entertainment). Dream big, right?

A year later, we had a teaser to show for it.

## The teaser

**January 1, 2021.** I put the *GLITCH* teaser trailer on my YouTube channel.

Runtime: **2 minutes, 34 seconds**.

![The word GLITCH in silver letters on a black screen, the title card of the teaser](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-title.jpg)
*The title card. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI).*

The credits:

- **Written and directed by** Garrett Warren
- **Edited by** Dan Liu (he did the sound design too)
- **Produced by** me
- **Starring** me, Samuel Jaye Paul and Matt Shadden
- **Original score by** PARKRow

You might know Dan from my [Bond music video post](https://blog.mckaystewart.com/2026/10/15/i-made-a-bond-music-video-with-dan-liu/). He's one of my go-to editors.

In my post, I wrote "RIP" next to Matt's name. He had passed away. This teaser is one way his work lives on.

## What happens in it

It's a fight. In a parking garage. At night.

Cold blue light. Concrete pillars. A white van.

It opens on a grainy security camera.

![Grainy black-and-white security camera view of an empty parking garage with a timecode in the corner](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-security-cam.jpg)
*The security cam open. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI).*

Then the guys in tank tops and suspenders show up.

And the hoodie gets to work.

![A fighter in a white tank top and suspenders squares up in a dark parking garage](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-garage-fight.jpg)
*Squaring up. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI).*

![The hooded fighter throws a man into the side of a white van in the garage](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-van-fight.jpg)
*Into the van. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI).*

![Close shot of a muscular fighter in a tank top grappling with the hooded fighter beside the van](/assets/images/2026-10-18/glitch-fight-close.jpg)
*Close quarters. Frame from the [GLITCH teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uGJ_VsZp0dI).*

Fights like this look simple. They're not.

Every hit is planned. Every fall is rehearsed. The camera has to be in the right spot, or the punch looks fake.

That's Garrett's specialty.

## What came next

The teaser was only the start of working with Garrett.

**April 1, 2021.** I dropped a new action and stunt demo reel. I thanked Garrett first.

**August 21, 2021.** I did a stunt driving tune-up with **@motionpicturedrivingclinic**. In my post I thanked Garrett and **@unltdvp** for hiring me to drive.

Then came the big one.

## *Avatar: The Way of Water*

**December 16, 2022.** *Avatar: The Way of Water* hit theaters.

Garrett was a **stunt coordinator** and a **second unit director** on it.

And I'm in the credits too. The [American Film Institute listing](https://watch.afi.com/movie/avatar-2) has me under **Utility Stunts**.

**December 26, 2022.** I saw it and posted: "Avatar 2 stunning visuals, worth seeing in IMAX 3D. Thank you @garrettxwarren for the job on it. Was surreal."

Surreal is the right word. Three years earlier I was posting goals about James Cameron's company. Now I was working on his movie.
