Holy moly. Back in 2015 my old Blogspot had exactly two posts, and they were both about the same crazy little movie: **West Side Swordy**. Post one was the trailer. Post two was me announcing the website. Eleven years later I'm reviving the blog, so it felt right to start with the project I was yelling about back then, and to finally tell the whole thing in one place.

Oh, and the website is back from the dead too. More on that below.

![West Side Swordy poster](/assets/images/2026-10-08/wss-poster.jpg)
*West Side Swordy poster art, from the 2015 press kit (poster designs credited to Alex Lombardo and Julian Martin).*

## So what IS West Side Swordy?

Picture *West Side Story*. Now give everybody swords. Now put Deadmau5 in charge.

That's basically it. Here's the official synopsis from our 2015 press kit:

> "The Jets chase the Sharks through an alleyway and up over a tall fence, when suddenly they find themselves in another world, magically transported into an abandoned warehouse. Where they are controlled by the electronic beat of the world-renowned disc jockey, Deadmau5. This is an LA West Side Story."

IMDb's one-liner is even better: *"The 'Jets' chase the 'Sharks' over a fence and land in... a warehouse! With swords in their hands. And Deadmau5 controls them all!"*

It's a 9-minute short (IMDb lists it as a 2015 U.S. short, genres Short / Action / Adventure), made under my company, Origins of Light, and shot in Los Angeles.

## My jobs on it (spoiler: a lot of them)

Per IMDb's full credits, I:

- **wrote** it,
- **produced** it (executive producer / producer),
- **played Riff**, leader of the Jets,
- and got a **co-choreographer** credit.

And I did not do it alone, not even close. **Dan Liu** directed and edited. **John T. Connor** and **Nate Fu** shot it. **Nick Lanzisera** choreographed. **Erin Rogers** did costumes. **Jason Brillantes** played Bernardo (and did stunts), and **Jaimie Bess** played Anita. Deadmau5 himself was played by **Saige Walker** and **André Van Driessche**. The rest of the Jets, Sharks and dancers are all on the [IMDb credits page](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3185554/fullcredits), and every single one of them killed it.

Why swords? In my own words from the 2015 press kit:

> "I originally wanted to create a sword-fighting reel to try and help me and a few fellow stunt performers get jobs on the Pirates of the Caribbean movies. No joke! But then, I wanted it to be special, to have real production value, have a story. In essence, a short film."

And why *West Side Story*? Also from the press kit: I'd performed in it twice, as Baby John, once in Austin, Texas and once in Redondo Beach. Definitely my favorite musical, by far.

## The website (May 24, 2015)

Post number two, word for word:

> "Over one year in the making and hundreds of hours of labor, it is finally finished. www.westsideswordy.com !"

Two exclamation points in the title. I was *pumped*.

And honestly? It deserved it. The site was built by **Halo Creative**, a Los Angeles design firm, and it was gorgeous: an illustrated, parallax warehouse world with my big ol' sword and the mau5 head floating out front, a pop-up trailer, a cast "About" page you unlocked by playing a memory game, an RSVP form for the invitation-only world premiere showcase, and the song "Pandora" by composer Pascal Tatipata playing in the background. The homepage even said "Funded in part by Kickstarter."

What I remember most is that the whole thing **moved when you tilted your phone**. The layers shifted with you, like you were peeking around inside the warehouse. That's not just my memory, either. The archived code runs the scene through Parallax.js, a plugin that drives the layers "from the gyroscope output of a smartdevice" (and falls back to your mouse on a computer). Our Awwwards entry bragged about "Gyroscope and mouse-activated parallax animations" and put the build at "over 500 hours of interactive blood, sweat and tears over a period of nine months."

### It got award love

The design community noticed:

- **Awwwards Nominee**, August 11, 2015 ([awwwards.com/sites/west-side-swordy](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/west-side-swordy)). That's the little "Nominee" ribbon you can see in the top-left corner of the screenshot.
- **CSSReel Nominee** ([cssreel.com/website/west-side-swordy](https://cssreel.com/website/west-side-swordy/)).
- Featured in the **CSS Nectar** gallery on July 23, 2015 ([cssnectar.com](https://cssnectar.com/css-gallery-inspiration/west-side-swordy/)).

### And now it's back!

The original domain died a long time ago. But the Internet Archive saved most of it, so I've rebuilt it from the Wayback captures. Go play with it:

**[Visit the restored westsideswordy.com](/westsideswordy/)**

On a computer, move your mouse around. On a phone, turn it sideways and tilt it (on an iPhone, tap once and allow motion access). A few things didn't survive: the photo gallery images were never archived, and the RSVP and contact forms are closed now. But the warehouse, the trailer pop-up, the music and the tilt are all back.

## Where it went from there

West Side Swordy got out into the world:

- **Dances With Films 2016** listed it as a **World Premiere**, screening in the "Downbeat" program on Friday, June 3 at 2:45 PM ([listing](https://danceswithfilms.com/west-side-swordy/)).
- The **2016 Urban Mediamakers Film Festival** (UMFF) named it **Best International Music-Driven Short Film** ([UMFF 2016 winners, PDF](https://umff.com/assets/pdf/umff-winners-2016.pdf)).
- The **Short Stop International Film Festival** programmed it in a music video block ([listing](https://www.shortstopfest.com/music-video-4)).

Holy moly. A sword-fighting reel I wanted to send to the *Pirates* people turned into a short with a Technocrane, a Deadmau5 head, an award-nominated website and a festival win. Thank you to every single person who bled, sweated and danced on this thing.
