**Spring 2021.** Hollywood was still shut down. No red carpets. No parties.

So I went digging through my old photos.

Over about **10 weeks**, I posted **five** throwbacks on X. A SAG Awards backdrop. A selfie with **Roy E. Disney**. A Golden Globes night with **Amy Adams** and **Common**. And a premiere party with Paramount's **Brad Grey**.

Here's the story behind each one. Let's start with my favorite.

![McKay Stewart in a black tux and vest smiling next to Amy Adams in a black strapless gown at a Golden Globes after-party](/assets/images/2026-10-27/amy-adams.jpg)
*With Amy Adams at a Golden Globes after-party. From my [X post on March 8, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1369003784509227009).*

## Amy Adams at the Globes

**March 8, 2021.** I posted two photos from the NBC/Universal Golden Globes after-party.

I wrote: "If I look happy it's cause I am."

I was. In my post I called her "talented and super sweet." I also said I hoped to work with her someday. Still true.

Her record speaks for itself:

- **2** Golden Globe wins, for ***American Hustle*** and ***Big Eyes***
- **6** Oscar nominations, from ***Junebug*** to ***Vice***

I didn't say which year this was. So I won't guess here.

The same day, I posted a second shot. It's me alone on the red carpet, in front of the NBC, Universal and Focus Features logos. That one has a photo agency watermark on it. So I'm [linking to it on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1368998719962681349) instead of copying it here.

## Why that party mattered

For years, NBC aired the Golden Globes. And NBCUniversal and Focus Features threw one of the big after-parties of the night.

It ran at the **Beverly Hilton**, the same hotel as the show. Big tent. Big names. Lots of dancing.

My caption: "what a treat. Mas por favor!"

That's Spanish for "more, please." I meant it.

## Common, same night vibes

**March 21, 2021.** I posted one more from the Globes.

This time it was with **Common**. I called him "super nice, super chill." He even threw up a peace sign for the photo.

![McKay Stewart in a black tux giving a thumbs-up next to Common in a white suit flashing a peace sign](/assets/images/2026-10-27/common.jpg)
*With Common at the Golden Globes. From my [X post on March 21, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1373721517620654095).*

If you only know him as a rapper, here's the short list:

- Grammy wins, including **"Love of My Life"** with Erykah Badu
- The **2015** Golden Globe and Oscar for Best Original Song, for **"Glory"** from ***Selma***
- Movie roles in ***American Gangster***, ***Wanted***, ***Terminator Salvation*** and ***John Wick: Chapter 2***

In ***Selma***, he also played civil rights leader **James Bevel**.

Music and movies. Both at the top. And still chill at a party.

## Roy Disney at the El Capitan

**March 1, 2021.** I posted a selfie with **Roy E. Disney**.

It was taken at the premiere of ***Morning Light*** at the **El Capitan Theatre** in Hollywood. I wrote that he was "super friendly and kind." He was.

![McKay Stewart smiling in a selfie next to Roy E. Disney, who wears a Hawaiian shirt and a white flower lei](/assets/images/2026-10-27/roy-disney-selfie.jpg)
*My selfie with Roy E. Disney at the Morning Light premiere. From my [X post on March 1, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1366468308950798340).*

Look at that lei. There's a reason for it.

***Morning Light*** is a Disney documentary about young sailors. **15** of them trained for **6 months**. Then **11** raced a 52-foot boat in the **Transpac**, from Los Angeles to Honolulu, Hawaii.

Roy and his wife, **Leslie DeMeuse**, came up with the idea. They also produced it.

The premiere was **October 7, 2008**, at the El Capitan. The film opened in theaters **10 days** later.

## Who was Roy E. Disney?

He was **Walt Disney's nephew**. His dad, **Roy O. Disney**, co-founded the studio with Walt.

Roy E. worked with the company for **56 years**. From **1984 to 2003**, he was vice chairman of the board and ran the animation department.

That's the era of:

- ***The Little Mermaid***
- ***Beauty and the Beast***
- ***Aladdin***
- ***The Lion King***

People call it Disney's animation comeback. He was a big part of it.

He was also a serious sailor. That's why he made ***Morning Light***.

Roy E. Disney died on **December 16, 2009**. He was **79**. I'm glad I got to say hi.

## Brad Grey at Benjamin Button

**April 22, 2021.** I posted a photo with **Brad Grey**.

I met him at the after-party for ***The Curious Case of Benjamin Button***. I called him "such a nice guy." I also gave a shoutout to **Brad Pitt** and **Tilda Swinton**, who were both in the film.

![McKay Stewart smiling in a black tux and vest beside Brad Grey in a bow tie at a dark after-party](/assets/images/2026-10-27/brad-grey.jpg)
*With Paramount chairman and CEO Brad Grey at the Benjamin Button after-party. From my [X post on April 22, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1385252113257844745).*

The Los Angeles premiere was **December 8, 2008**, at the Village Theater in Westwood. It was black tie.

*Variety* asked Grey why. He said: "It's always good to see Brad Pitt in a tux."

The after-party was in a big see-through tent in Century City.

## Who was Brad Grey?

He ran **Paramount Pictures** as chairman and CEO from **2005 to 2017**. That's **12 years**.

Before that, he co-founded the management company **Brillstein-Grey** with Bernie Brillstein. Today it's called Brillstein Entertainment Partners.

He also produced TV. His shows include ***The Sopranos*** and ***The Larry Sanders Show***.

He died on **May 14, 2017**. He was **59**.

## The SAG Awards

**February 14, 2021.** This was the first of the five posts.

It's me in front of the **Screen Actors Guild Awards** wall. Thumbs up. Big grin.

![McKay Stewart in a dark suit and striped tie giving a thumbs-up in front of a Screen Actors Guild Awards backdrop](/assets/images/2026-10-27/sag-awards.jpg)
*At the SAG Awards. From my [X post on February 14, 2021](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1361032209923248128).*

My caption: "Actors love supporting actors, such a great vibe!"

That's the whole idea of this show. It's actors voting for actors.

A few facts:

- The first SAG Awards were on **February 25, 1995**, live on NBC
- The statuette is called **The Actor**
- Since **November 2025**, the show is called **The Actor Awards presented by SAG-AFTRA**

I also wrote that I looked forward to going again "when the craziness ends." In early 2021, that meant the pandemic.

## Same tux, many nights

Look closely at the Amy Adams, Common and Brad Grey photos.

Black tux. Black vest. Black tie.

A good tux earns its keep. Mine worked hard.

## Why I'm sharing these

Red carpets are not the job. The job is the set. The fights. The falls. The scenes.

But these nights matter too. You get to thank people. You meet folks who built the business. Some of them, like Roy and Brad, aren't here anymore.

When I posted these, I couldn't go anywhere. So I looked back and smiled.

I hashtagged one of them **#LuckyMe**. Still feels right.
