**June 2017.** Los Angeles.

I needed help for a secret job.

I posted that I was looking for background for a music video. The director was **Dan Liu**. He had already directed my short *West Side Swordy*. By then he was also directing on ***The Walking Dead***.

I asked online if anyone could help me learn Arabic. The job was set to start **July 15**.

I did not say what the video was.

I tagged it later with one word that gave it away: **#bondjamesbond**.

![McKay Stewart in a dark suit and black tie standing on a wooden fence overlooking Los Angeles](/assets/images/2026-10-15/bond-overlook.jpg)
*Me in the Bond look above Los Angeles. Photo from the #guerillasinthemidst shoot I [shared on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/896067770680434688).*

## The secret project

We called it a **super secret project**.

Hashtags I used:

- **#topsecret**
- **#supersecretproject**
- **#guerillasinthemidst**
- **#bondjamesbond**
- **#BTS**

Guerilla means you move fast. Small crew. Real streets. No giant studio gates.

That was us.

The song was my cover of **"Never Stop"** by **Safety Suit** (Wedding Version). Unofficial. I sang it. **Tea McKay** acted in it.

I had spent about **7 years** in musical theatre. I wanted to get back to singing. This was my shot.

## El Matador, Malibu

**August 6, 2017.** Second-to-last location of the shoot.

I posted a smiling set photo and called it **#topsecret** and **#BTS**.

![McKay Stewart and Tea McKay smiling on location during the Bond music video shoot](/assets/images/2026-10-15/bts-location-smile.jpg)
*On set with Tea McKay. Photo: McKay Stewart, [shared on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/894237528378572802).*

**August 7.** We hit **El Matador Beach** in Malibu.

Cliffs. Rocks. Sunset light. Classic Bond beach energy.

I posted more "top secret bts" from there. The ocean stills from the finished video show why we went.

![McKay Stewart and Tea McKay at El Matador Beach at sunset during the Never Stop music video](/assets/images/2026-10-15/el-matador-sunset.jpg)
*El Matador at sunset, from the finished video. Frame from [Never Stop (my cover)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ch5tbUxQ9r8).*

## Night shoot over the city

**August 11.** Night. Downtown glowing behind us.

I posted three photos with the Bond hashtags. One is black and white. Suit. Bow tie. Tea beside me. The skyline soft in the back.

![McKay Stewart in a tuxedo with Tea McKay on a Los Angeles street at night, downtown skyline behind them, black and white](/assets/images/2026-10-15/bond-skyline-couple.jpg)
*Guerilla night look with Tea McKay and the LA skyline. Photo from the same [Aug 11 BTS post](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/896067770680434688).*

Another shot shows the real work: a yellow hatchback with the hatch up. Camera on a low rig. Monitor lit. **Dan Liu** standing nearby in a jacket that says "FIND ME IN THE FUTURE."

![Behind the scenes night shoot with Dan Liu and a cinema camera on a Los Angeles hillside overlooking downtown](/assets/images/2026-10-15/bond-bts-night.jpg)
*BTS: camera in the trunk, city lights behind us. Dan Liu on the left. Photo from the same [Aug 11 post](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/896067770680434688).*

Small budget. Big hearts. That was the pitch then. It still is.

## Who made it

Core credits from the YouTube upload:

- **Director:** Dan Liu (then of *The Walking Dead*; he also directed *West Side Swordy* for me)
- **Artist / singer:** me, McKay Stewart
- **Actress:** Tea McKay
- **Scoring:** Leo Winokur
- **Recording artist / engineer:** Jaidan Millar

Later I shouted out **John Connor** (our DP energy from the *West Side Swordy* days) and photographer **Elizabeth Kingston Young** for great stills from the project.

Dan kept climbing after this. He went on to direct more *Walking Dead*, then shows like ***For All Mankind***, ***Star Trek: Strange New Worlds***, and ***Star Trek: Picard***.

I got to make a Bond-style music video with him on LA streets first.

## The video drops

**October 14, 2017.** The secret was out.

Runtime: about **3 minutes** (**181** seconds).

I posted the YouTube link and wrote: "Music video out finally! Directed by @icechai Dan Liu of @thewalkingdead. Enjoy!"

In the description I asked people to support Safety Suit. Fair is fair. We used their song. We made our own pictures.

I also noted you could find me on the latest season of *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* and in films like *Eagle Eye* and *The Curious Case of Benjamin Button*.

Funny timing: **five days** before this video went live, on **October 9, 2017**, I booked my second *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* role — Roughneck. ([That fight story is here.](/2026/10/13/ming-na-wen-beat-me-up-on-agents-of-s-h-i-e-l-d/))

So that week I had a Bond music video dropping and a Marvel co-star booking. Wild week.

## Why I still like this one

It was not a studio check. It was friends, a small crew, a tuxedo, a beach, and a night street with the city behind us.

Dan Liu trusted the guerilla plan. Tea McKay showed up and made the frames sing. Leo and Jaidan helped the music land.

I got to sing again.

And for a few nights in August 2017, Los Angeles was our Bond set.
