**November 13, 2014.** Evening, Pacific time.

I typed one line and hit send:

> "Just booked a role on @Marvel @agentsofshield #agentsofshield! #Boom"

That's it. No photo. No details. Just **#Boom**.

A week earlier I was in Seattle rehearsing fights with Manu Bennett on *Beta Test* ([that post](/2026/10/11/holy-moly-i-rehearsed-with-manu-bennett-on-beta-test/)). Now I was going into the **Marvel** universe.

My part: **Scientist #1**. The episode: **"Aftershocks."** Season **2**, Episode **11** of ***Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.***

Here's the story, plus every frame of it I've shared over the years.

## What is "Aftershocks"?

***Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*** ran on **ABC** for **7 seasons**. It follows **Phil Coulson** (Clark Gregg) and his team of agents inside the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

"Aftershocks" aired on **March 3, 2015**. It picks up right after a huge mid-season finale.

Quick setup:

- Skye (**Chloe Bennet**) just got earthquake powers in an alien city under the ground
- Raina (**Ruth Negga**) changed in the same temple, and she came out covered in thorns
- Coulson wants payback on Hydra
- Jemma Simmons (**Elizabeth Henstridge**) is getting ready to flood the alien city so nobody else goes in

That last part is where I come in.

The crew working down in that city wears white hazmat suits. Lights. Air hoses. Tablets. Cameras. Then Raina crawls out of the rubble and attacks the team.

Scientist #1 does not have a good night. When I posted about it later, I tagged it **#deathtomckay**. That should tell you how it ends for my guy.

## Who made it

The people behind this episode are a big deal in the Marvel TV world:

- **Written by:** Maurissa Tancharoen and Jed Whedon (the show's executive producers)
- **Directed by:** Billy Gierhart
- **Original title:** "Epidemic," before Marvel renamed it

When Marvel.com announced the guest cast for the episode, my name was on the list: **McKay Stewart as scientist #1**. That list also had **Kyle MacLachlan**, **Ruth Negga**, **Adrian Pasdar**, **Henry Simmons** and **Dichen Lachman**.

Ruth Negga got an **Oscar nomination** two years later for *Loving*. So yes, I got taken out by a future Oscar nominee. I'll take it.

## The shoot day

The shoot was on **Wednesday, November 26, 2014**. That's the day before Thanksgiving.

On Thanksgiving morning I posted:

> "Great working with you yesterday @Lil_Henstridge!!"

I also gave a **#yourebrilliant** shout-out to **Iain De Caestecker**, who plays Leo Fitz.

Fitz and Simmons were fan favorites on that show. Getting a day in their world meant a lot.

Booked **November 13**. Shot **November 26**. That's under **2 weeks**. TV moves fast.

## The suit

Acting in a full hazmat suit is its own skill.

Your face sits behind a plastic visor. The hood and air hose hide most of you. So your body has to tell the story.

That's where stunt training helps. You hit your marks. You move like a real tech who does this every day. Then, when the scene turns, you sell it. Fast. Big. Clean.

## Air date

I counted down.

On **February 23, 2015** I posted: "See me on TV series #AgentsofSHIELD March 3rd, woot! Playing a scientist :D"

On **March 2, 2015**: "Catch me on #AgentsofSHIELD 'Aftershocks' tomorrow 8PM/9PMC! I play a scientist :)"

It aired on ABC on **Tuesday, March 3, 2015**. About **4.48 million** people watched that night, per Nielsen numbers reported by *TV by the Numbers*.

**4.48 million.** For a guy in a hazmat suit who said "#Boom" on Twitter a few months before.

The episode later went to **Netflix** (June 2015) and is now on **Disney+**.

## "Hey! Remember me?"

In **August 2016**, I sent Elizabeth Henstridge that camera still with a simple line: **"Hey! Remember me?"** Hashtags: **#aftershocks**, **#superscientist**, **#deathtomckay**.

Two days later, I was inviting people to a screening of my short ***West Side Swordy*** at the **TCL Chinese Theatres** in Hollywood ([the Swordy story](/2026/10/08/holy-moly-west-side-swordy/)).

Look at the invite. Under "Created by McKay Stewart" it says **(Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.)**. One day in a hazmat suit, and it was on my billing.

## Why this one matters

Look at my **2014** run:

- **April:** stunt doubling **Scott Wolf** on TNT's *Perception* ([that post](/2026/10/09/holy-moly-i-doubled-scott-wolf-on-perception/))
- **August:** wrapped *For Blood* as Dead Card Danny ([that post](/2026/10/10/holy-moly-i-played-dead-card-danny-in-for-blood/))
- **November:** Seattle with **Manu Bennett** on *Beta Test*
- **Late November:** Marvel. *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* "Aftershocks."

That's the working actor and stunt life. You train. You audition. You book. You show up and nail your day. Then you hit send on one line: **#Boom**.

And it wasn't my last trip to S.H.I.E.L.D. In **2021** I posted that I still couldn't get over working **2 roles** on the show. The second one came in Season **5**, and it had a fight with **Ming-Na Wen**. That's the next post in this series.
