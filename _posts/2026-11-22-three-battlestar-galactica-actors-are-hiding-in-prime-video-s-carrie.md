## The show

**Carrie** is a Prime Video original series. All **8** episodes dropped at once on **October 7, 2026**, in more than **240** countries and territories ([Amazon MGM Studios press](https://press.amazonmgmstudios.com/us/en/press-release/prime-video-sets-the-stage-at-san-diego-comiccon-w)).

It's the first time Stephen King's first novel has been made into a TV series.

Mike Flanagan is the showrunner. He wrote it, executive produced it, and directed **4** episodes. He's the filmmaker behind *The Haunting of Hill House* and *Midnight Mass*. Stephen King is an executive producer too.

Summer H. Howell plays Carrie White. She got the part after a search where more than **1,000** actors read for it. Samantha Sloyan plays her mother, Margaret.

## The BSG actors

Here's who I'm talking about.

- **Katee Sackhoff** played Kara "Starbuck" Thrace on Battlestar Galactica. In Carrie, she plays Amanda, Emaline's mother, and is credited in **2** episodes ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt11540908/fullcredits/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series))).
- **Tahmoh Penikett** played Karl "Helo" Agathon. In Carrie, he plays Ralph White, Carrie's late father, seen in flashbacks, and is credited in **6** episodes ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt11540908/fullcredits/)).
- **Michael Trucco** played Samuel Anders. Here he's John Hargensen, the dad of mean girl Chris Hargensen, credited in **6** episodes ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt11540908/fullcredits/)).

Flanagan told TV Insider he already had Penikett, Sackhoff and Trucco in the show ([TV Insider](https://www.tvinsider.com/1295125/carrie-battlestar-galactica-reunion-mike-flanagan-michael-trucco-tahmoh-penikett-katee-sackhoff/)).

## Battlestar Galactica, quick refresher

The reimagined Battlestar Galactica was developed by Ronald D. Moore. It started with a miniseries in **December 2003** on the Sci Fi Channel. Then came **4** regular seasons. The finale aired **March 20, 2009** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica_(2004_TV_series))).

On IMDb, Sackhoff is listed for **72** episodes as Starbuck and Penikett for **65** as Helo ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0407362/)).

Starbuck was a hotshot Viper pilot. Helo was a Raptor officer. Seeing them on screen in the same show again? That's the "together again kinda" in my caption.

## Matthew Lillard and the counselor

**Matthew Lillard** plays Principal Henry Grayle. He's a series regular, credited in all **8** episodes ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/288673-carrie/cast)). Kudos.

And "damn the counselor"? That's **Tim Bagley**. He plays Peter Morton, the guidance counselor at Ewen High School ([Deadline](https://deadline.com/lists/carrie-tv-show-cast-vs-movies/)).

Flanagan told Men's Health that Lillard and Bagley play two of the kindest characters in the show ([Men's Health](https://www.menshealth.com/entertainment/a74070271/carrie-mike-flanagan-interview-male-characters/)). In one scene, Morton and Carrie connect over books. Bagley said that was the scene he auditioned with ([PopHorror](https://pophorror.com/interview-with-tim-bagley-star-of-carrie-2026/)).

So good.

## The book that almost got thrown away

*Carrie* was Stephen King's first published novel. Doubleday released it on **April 5, 1974** ([StephenKing.com](https://stephenking.com/works/novel/carrie.html)). The first printing was **30,000** copies ([The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/stephen-king-interview-horror-novel-carrie-1236753241/)).

It almost never happened.

In his memoir *On Writing*, King says he wrote **3** single-spaced pages, crumpled them up and threw them away.

The next night, his wife **Tabitha King** had the pages. She found them while emptying his wastebasket. She shook off the cigarette ashes, smoothed them out and read them. She wanted to know the rest of the story ([The Guardian, excerpt from On Writing](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2000/sep/17/stephenking.fiction)).

He kept going.

Doubleday bought the book in **1973** for **$2,500**. The news came by telegram, because the Kings didn't have a phone ([The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/stephen-king-interview-horror-novel-carrie-1236753241/)).

## The 1976 movie

Two years later, Brian De Palma made it a movie. **Carrie** (1976) starred Sissy Spacek as Carrie and Piper Laurie as her mother.

Both got Academy Award nominations: Spacek for Best Actress, Laurie for Best Supporting Actress ([AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/55851-CARRIE)).

Now, **52** years after the book, Carrie is a TV series. With Starbuck, Helo and Samuel Anders in it.
