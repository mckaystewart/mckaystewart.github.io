**2021** was my first year learning to skate. You can read that story here: [From First Ollie to First Grind](/2026/10/26/from-first-ollie-to-first-grind-my-year-learning-to-skate-venice/).

**2022** was year two. I was still a beginner. I said so in almost every post: **#n00b. #beginner. #babysteps.**

But the baby steps got bigger. In one year I skated **4** parks in **3** states. I landed my first **50-50 grind**. And I spun my first **360** on rollerblades since college.

Here's how it went.

*Riding the bowl at Venice. Photo by @spangheromarco, from my [X post on February 4, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1489629598342017025).*

## January: Pedlow

**January 26, 2022.** I posted a clip from **Pedlow Skate Park** in Encino. My caption: "love this park!"

Pedlow has history. It was the **first** public skatepark run by the City of Los Angeles. It opened on **February 17, 2001**, per [LA Rec and Parks](https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/skatepark/pedlow-skate).

It has a big pool, a snake run, a pyramid and rails. Lots of room to learn.

In the clip, I carve the bowl, tap the coping, and air out at the end.

## Blades and a branny

**Two days later**, I was back at Pedlow. This time on rollerblades.

My friend **Holland Diaz** had shown me a flip called a **branny**. I went for it.

I didn't land it. Not yet. I turned it into a **roundoff** instead. That's a gymnastics move, hands down, feet over.

Then I fell. A few times. Then I got up and landed a basic grind on the coping.

My caption: "#nevergiveup #tryagain."

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/assets/images/2026-10-29/pedlow-blades-roundoff-jan-2022.jpg" style="width:100%;height:auto;"><source src="/assets/images/2026-10-29/pedlow-blades-roundoff-jan-2022.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>
*Working toward the branny on blades: roundoffs, fails, then a coping grind. From my [X post on January 28, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1487099765250674689).*

That's stunt work in a nutshell. Try. Fall. Adjust. Try again.

## The kiddie ramp

**February 10, 2022.** Back to Pedlow. This time I was on the **kiddie ramp**.

Yes, really. My caption: "#Coping #basics #progression on the kiddie ramp at Pedlow. Hope to graduate soon!"

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/assets/images/2026-10-29/pedlow-kiddie-ramp-feb-2022.jpg" style="width:100%;height:auto;"><source src="/assets/images/2026-10-29/pedlow-kiddie-ramp-feb-2022.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>
*Coping basics on the small ramp at Pedlow. And yes, one fall. From my [X post on February 10, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1491795325207543814).*

Here's a tip for anyone starting out. **Small ramps are not embarrassing.** They're where you build the moves you need for the big ones.

Coping is the metal edge at the top of a ramp. You learn to stall on it, grind on it, and drop in off it. Do it a hundred times low. Then take it higher.

## March: the snake run

**March 14, 2022.** This one's my favorite clip of the year.

It's a **fisheye** run through the **snake run** at Venice. A fisheye is a super-wide lens. It makes the bowl look huge and bendy.

My friend **@kylejetter** filmed it. And he did it while skating right behind me. My caption: "this is actually insanely hard."

*My best snake run to date, filmed fisheye by @kylejetter. From my [X post on March 14, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1503365633563451394).*

## June: Georgia and my first 50-50

**June 16, 2022.** New state. New park. I was skating in **Newnan, Georgia**.

My caption: "First 5050 grind! And some other baby steps at Newnan Skate Park GA."

A **50-50** is when both trucks grind along the edge at the same time. It's a basic. And it took me a while.

I also wrote that I "ate sh*t like 10 times." I wished I'd filmed the fails.

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/assets/images/2026-10-29/newnan-first-5050-jun-2022.jpg" style="width:100%;height:auto;"><source src="/assets/images/2026-10-29/newnan-first-5050-jun-2022.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>
*My first 50-50 grind, plus more baby steps, in Newnan, Georgia. From my [X post on June 16, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1537441184162803714).*

Newnan's skate park is newer. The city opened it at **C. Jay Smith Memorial Park** in **August 2021**. It's a skate park and pump track in one. The city called it the **largest** combo of its kind in Georgia ([The Newnan Times-Herald](https://www.times-herald.com/news/local/c-jay-smith-memorial-skate-park-to-open-friday/article_335328c8-4c74-5e28-a8fe-b354780dcbaa.html)).

## December: a Christmas air in Austin

**December 25, 2022.** Christmas Day. I was in **Austin, Texas**, at **House Park**.

My caption: "Merrry Christmasss Airrrr!"

![McKay Stewart in a red jacket and sunglasses catching air off the graffiti-covered bowl at House Park in Austin, Texas](/assets/images/2026-10-29/house-park-christmas-air-2022.jpg)
*A Christmas Day air at House Park in Austin. From my [X post on December 25, 2022](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1607149143654277123).*

House Park is right downtown. Its official name is the **Heath Eiland and Morgan Moss BMX Skate Park**. It opened on **June 16, 2011**, and it covers **30,000** square feet, per the [City of Austin](https://services.austintexas.gov/department/skate-parks).

Not a bad way to spend a holiday.

## What year two taught me

Here's my 2022 in numbers:

- **4** parks: Pedlow, Venice, Newnan, House Park
- **3** states: California, Georgia, Texas
- **1** first 50-50 grind
- **1** first 360 on blades since college
- Lots of falls. I didn't count.

I'm an actor and a stuntman. My job is to make hard things look easy.

Skating reminds me that nothing starts easy. You start on the kiddie ramp. You fall ten times. You try again.

And if you're lucky, a friend films it.
