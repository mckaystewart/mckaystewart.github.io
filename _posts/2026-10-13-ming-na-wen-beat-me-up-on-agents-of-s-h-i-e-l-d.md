**October 9, 2017.** Lunchtime in Los Angeles.

I typed one quick post:

> "Just booked co-star role on @AgentsofSHIELD, whoop!!"

This was my **second** trip into the world of ***Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*** Back in 2015 I played Scientist #1 in a hazmat suit ([that story is here](/2026/10/12/i-played-scientist-1-on-agents-of-s-h-i-e-l-d/)).

This time I got a knife, a scarf, and a fight with **Ming-Na Wen**.

Spoiler: I lost.

![McKay Stewart as Roughneck, in a scarf and tactical vest, standing beside another man in a dark corridor in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. "The Last Day"](/assets/images/2026-10-13/roughneck-standoff.jpg)
*Me (right) as Roughneck in "The Last Day" (ABC, 2018). Still from Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., from the clip I [shared on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/1372573387206184961).*

## The role: Roughneck

My character's name was **Roughneck**.

The episode was **"The Last Day."** That's Season **5**, Episode **8**. It aired on **ABC** on **Friday, January 19, 2018**.

Season 5 was a wild one. The team got pulled into the future. Earth was broken into pieces. Most of them were stuck on a space station called the Lighthouse.

In "The Last Day," part of the team crash-lands on what's left of Earth. Somewhere in that mess, Coulson (**Clark Gregg**) and May (**Ming-Na Wen**) run into my guy.

A hometown paper asked me about Roughneck that month. Here's how I put it:

> "He's a guy that's in over his head and trying to do what he thinks is right. He gets caught up in a confrontation with actors Clark Gregg and Ming-Na Wen. He doesn't die though, so maybe he comes back."

## Who made it happen

When I booked it, I gave a shout to the people who helped:

- **Elizabeth Henstridge**, who plays Jemma Simmons on the show
- **Tanner Gill**
- **Stunts Unlimited**, the stunt group I tagged with **#theblackandgold**

The episode was directed by **Nina Lopez-Corrado**. In one post I wrote, "thanks for being so awesome Nina!"

## Shoot day

**October 17, 2017.** The day before.

I posted that I'd be an "actor/stuntman on @AgentsofSHIELD tomorrow. Wish me luck."

So that's the job. Say the lines. Then take the hits.

The costume was pure end-of-the-world. Worn tactical vest. Gloves. A big tan scarf. A knife.

In my trailer I couldn't help myself:

That December, I sent Ming-Na a note. I told her it was great fighting her. Then I fixed it: "I mean getting a beat down."

That's stunt work. You make the hero look great. You take the fall. You get up and do it again.

## Christmas lunch with S.H.I.E.L.D.

**December 12, 2017.** I got invited back.

Kyle Jewell asked me to come back to S.H.I.E.L.D. I got to watch him work. I called him a **"#digitalmaster"** and I meant it.

Then came the cast and crew Christmas lunch. It was big:

- **Sashimi**
- **Lobster**
- **Filet mignon**

I saw Nina Lopez-Corrado again. I met directors **Garry A. Brown** and **Billy Gierhart**. I saw Ming-Na across the room too, but I didn't get to say hi.

## Air night

**January 19, 2018.** Show night.

I posted this picture with a big thumbs-up:

![McKay Stewart giving a thumbs-up next to a TV showing the Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. logo](/assets/images/2026-10-13/roughneck-airs-tonight.jpg)
*"Watch me on @AgentsofSHIELD tonight at 9PM on ABC!" Photo: McKay Stewart, [shared on X](https://x.com/mckaystewart/status/954499199990513664).*

My post said it was a "super fun scene w @MingNa and @clarkgregg." It was.

How many people watched? The episode drew **2.41 million** viewers on air night. With a week of DVR viewing, that grew to **4.23 million**, per TV by the Numbers ratings cited on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D._season_5).

That's a lot of people watching me lose a fight.

## The hometown paper

Before the episode aired, the ***Vashon-Maury Island Beachcomber*** ran a story on me. It came out **January 16, 2018**.

Why Vashon? I lived on Vashon Island, Washington, from **1989 to 1992**. I went to Vashon High School.

The story covered a lot:

- My first acting was in **Sheila Jaffe's** English class. She's one of my best teachers of all time.
- After Vashon I went to Bellarmine Prep in Tacoma.
- Then I studied dance at **Loyola Marymount University** in Los Angeles.
- I danced in commercials and big musical shows for almost **7 years**.
- Then I started doing stunts. By 2018 I'd worked as a stuntman and actor on close to **70** TV shows and movies.

Some of those: *Desperate Housewives*, *The Curious Case of Benjamin Button*, *Million Dollar Baby*, and *CSI: Miami*.

I told the paper I miss Vashon. I played soccer, baseball and tennis. I wrestled. I acted. I learned French. I was on the debate team. It was a rocket ride.

You can read it here: [Former islander lands part in ABC series](https://www.vashonbeachcomber.com/life/former-islander-lands-part-in-abc-series/).

## Two roles, one show

Years later, in **March 2021**, I was still smiling about it.

I posted: "I still can't get over how great it was to work **2 roles** on Agents of SHIELD w cast/crew."

Two roles. Two seasons, three years apart:

- **Scientist #1** in "Aftershocks" (Season 2, 2015)
- **Roughneck** in "The Last Day" (Season 5, 2018)

One got me a hazmat suit. The other got me beat up by Melinda May.

Both were so much fun.
